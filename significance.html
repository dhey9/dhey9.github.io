The Significance of Pax Silica

Pax Silica is significant because its development could affect more than the Philippines' technology sector. Its proposed industrial activities could have major implications on farmers, local communities, natural resources, and the environment.

🌾

Impact on Farmers

Agricultural land could be converted for industrial use, potentially affecting local food production. Changes in nearby land could disrupt farming communities. Farmers could face changes in access to farmland and water resources, and might need support when development affects their livelihood.

🌿

Environment

Large industrial facilities will have an increased demand for water and energy. Industrial activity could also create additional risks: waste, pollution, and habitat destruction from mining and mineral processing. Environmental safeguards along with proper assessments and monitoring would ensure safety for the locals and indigenous people.

🤝

Community Participation

Local communities should receive clear information about the developments due to the concerns that will be spoken about by the neighboring communities because of the decisions involving the land and its resources due to the development of an industrial hub.

🏛️

Government Accountability

Government agencies have an important role in monitoring the environmental and social impacts of this initiative. Projects should follow the environmental regulations, while proper checks and consultations help ensure compliance. Clear communication between the government, businesses, and affected communities is important for addressing concerns all throughout the development process.

📢 Our Overall Advocacy

"Our advocacy is to protect Filipino farmers and communities, demand stronger environmental safeguards, promote government accountability, and ensure that national development benefits Filipinos without compromising the land, resources, and future."
