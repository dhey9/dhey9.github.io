🌐 Background of Pax Silica

Understanding the Context, Concepts, and Foundations

Pax Silica is the Department of State's leading strategic initiative to build a secure and innovation-driven silicon supply chain. Launched in December 2025, it advances a new economic security consensus among allies and trusted stakeholders, making technology supply chains more resilient and less vulnerable to disruptions or coercive dependencies.

The name combines pax (Latin for peace and stability) and silica (silicon) — the key material used in semiconductor manufacturing.

🔑

Key Concepts & Definitions

  • Pax Silica: A US-led initiative promoting secure semiconductor supply chains through international cooperation.
  • Silicon / Silica: A key material used in semiconductor manufacturing and advanced technologies.
  • Semiconductor: Electronic components essential for modern devices, from phones to AI systems.
  • Economic Security Zone: A designated industrial area for strategic technology development.
🌍

Context & Environment

Pax Silica emerged amid growing global concerns about technology supply chain vulnerabilities, particularly the heavy dependence on concentrated sources of semiconductors. The initiative responds to geopolitical tensions, trade disruptions, and the strategic importance of chips in modern economies.

In the Philippines, the initiative aligns with the country's existing role in the global electronics industry and aims to position New Clark City as a major hub for AI, semiconductors, and advanced manufacturing.

🏗️

Prerequisites & Foundational Elements

  • International Cooperation: Participation from allied nations committed to supply chain security.
  • Investment Commitments: Joint funding and resource-sharing among partner countries.
  • Infrastructure Development: Facilities like the 4,000-acre Economic Security Zone in the Luzon Economic Corridor.
  • Policy Frameworks: Agreements such as the Pax Silica Declaration signed in December 2025.
🔗

Related Topics & Connections

  • Global Semiconductor Industry – Pax Silica directly engages with the worldwide chip supply chain.
  • Artificial Intelligence – AI development depends heavily on semiconductor availability.
  • Economic Security – The initiative is part of a broader push for resilient national economies.
  • Philippine Industrialization – Connects to the country's goals in advanced manufacturing and tech exports.
