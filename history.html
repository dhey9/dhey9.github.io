📜 History of Pax Silica in the Philippines

A Timeline of Events, Decisions, and Growing Debates

The Philippines' involvement in Pax Silica began in April 2026, when the country officially joined the U.S.-led initiative focused on semiconductor and critical-technology supply chains. Since then, the project has sparked both excitement about economic opportunities and growing concerns from farmers, indigenous communities, youth, and civic organizations.

🗺️ Why Was the Philippines Chosen for Pax Silica?

The Philippines brings a combination of resource endowment, industrial capability, and strategic positioning that makes it a natural fit for the Pax Silica network. As a close U.S. treaty ally, the country brings to Pax Silica key capabilities and human talent in technology manufacturing, including semiconductors and electronics.

💡 The Philippines' strong existing role in the global electronics industry, combined with its strategic location and alliance with the United States, positions it as a key partner in building more resilient technology supply chains.
