🔬 Pax Silica

Building a Secure and Resilient Silicon Supply Chain

Pax Silica is a US-led strategic initiative launched in December 2025 to make technology supply chains more secure, resilient, and less vulnerable to disruptions. The name combines pax (Latin for peace) and silica (silicon) — reflecting its goal of bringing stability to the global semiconductor industry through international cooperation.

💻

What is Pax Silica?

  • US-led technology initiative – focuses on building a more secure and reliable supply chain for important technologies.
  • Launched in December 2025 – created to make technology supply chains less vulnerable to disruptions and dependence on certain countries.
  • Focuses on silicon and semiconductors – silicon is an important material used to produce semiconductors and other advanced technologies.
  • Aims to strengthen cooperation – encourages participating countries to work together on technology, investment, and innovation.
🎯

Why Was Pax Silica Created?

  • Reduce dependence on vulnerable supply chains – helps countries avoid relying too heavily on limited sources for important technology materials.
  • Encourage investment and joint projects – promotes partnerships and investments between participating countries.
  • Build more resilient technology supply chains – aims to make the production and movement of technology more stable during disruptions.
  • Promote technological development – encourages countries to cooperate in developing new technologies and industries.
🗺️

Pax Silica & the Philippines

  • Philippines joined on April 17, 2026 – the country became the 13th signatory to the Pax Silica Declaration at the time.
  • 4,000-acre Economic Security Zone – the Philippines and the US announced plans for a large industrial area within the Luzon Economic Corridor.
  • New Clark City as a technology hub – the area is planned to support AI, semiconductors, advanced manufacturing, and related industries.
  • Expected economic opportunities – the project is intended to attract investments, create jobs, increase exports, and expand the country's technology sector.
🔮

Looking Ahead

  • Project is still developing – many parts of the proposed industrial hub are still being planned and assessed.
  • Possible effects on communities and agriculture – the government is studying how the project could affect surrounding areas and communities.
  • Development will happen in phases – the industrial hub is expected to expand gradually over the coming years.
  • Raises questions about safeguards – the project brings up questions about who benefits from the development and how its possible costs will be addressed.
